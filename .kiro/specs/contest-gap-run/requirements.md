Terms. **Gap id** = block id in `docs/WORK_QUEUE_20261115.md`. **Honest status** = one of done, partly, not done, not used, each backed by a file or a command output. **Export-visible** = the path exists and the export manifest (`scripts/release/export_manifest.json`) does not EXCLUDE it, so a judge can open it in the public repository.

## Stage 1 (pass/fail)
1. **Built with Kiro.** The submission shall show how Kiro was used in the eight topics of the rules (IDE/CLI/Web/Crew, custom agents, hooks, specs, steering, MCP, property-based tests, powers), each with an honest status and an export-visible path. A topic with no evidence shall say "not used" or "not done" and shall not be implied. (Gaps B01, B02, B12. Check: "every topic row has a status and its paths are export-visible")
2. **Public repository.** When the repository is made public, it shall contain `/.kiro` at the root and not ignore it, an MIT licence file, all source, assets and instructions. (B10, B11. Owner and main line; see "Not for Kiro".)
3. **Working URL.** The submission shall give a URL to the working app, free and open until 2027-01-29, with testing instructions for judges. (B03. The text half is a Kiro task; the deploy is owner work.)
4. **Demo video.** The video shall be under three minutes, public on YouTube and shown working on its device, with no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music. (B16. Kiro drafts the shot list only.)
5. **English materials.** The description, testing instructions and video text shall be in English. While the game text is Traditional Chinese, the materials shall say so and shall not claim an English interface. (B09, B13)
6. **New project and disclosure.** The write-up shall list every library, font, CC0 sound and generated picture, and any work that existed before 2026-10-30, so a judge can see what was not built during the Submission Period. Unknown provenance shall be listed as unknown. (B14)

## Stage 2 (one requirement per criterion)
7. **Potential Impact.** The description shall state in two or three sentences what problem the game and its agent seat solve, for whom, and why it matters, using only facts the files support. (B13, B16. Check: "pitch lines cite a file or a command")
8. **Technological Implementation.** The submission shall show a strategy and a variety of Kiro tools, each with evidence a judge can open: the verified ledger, specs, steering, hooks, agents, MCP state, powers, property tests, and what was not used. The evidence page shall say plainly what failed (for example the IDE MCP call). (B01, B02, B12. Check: "evidence counts come from a command, not from memory")
9. **Design.** The page shall not show gaps a judge can see: missing pictures, missing sounds, untranslated text, unfinished heroes. Where a gap cannot be closed before the deadline, the reference shall be mapped to a fix or dropped by the main line. (B07, B08, B09, B21. Check: `python3 -B scripts/release/check_asset_refs.py` missing count)
10. **Innovation.** The write-up shall point to what is new and visible: an agent that takes a seat through MCP from public information only, one-match learning, an adaptive public level, the hidden-point shield. Anything not yet in the page shall be marked as not in the page. (B05, B20)
11. **Presentation.** The video and text shall show the project working end to end, with a clear pitch, and shall not show a fallback, error or untranslated screen. (B16, B03. Kiro: outline only.)

## Process
12. **One task at a time, with proof.** Each Kiro task shall name the files it may touch, a command that proves it, the expected value and a result file of at most 8 lines. A task shall be ticked only when the command output is pasted in that result file. If a command is refused, the task stays unticked. (Check: the result files)
13. **Boundaries.** Kiro shall not edit `index.html`, `.gitignore` or the export tree, shall not push, deploy, call the network, or put private values, credit numbers or personal names in any file. Work that needs those, an account or the owner's own words shall be listed under "Not for Kiro" with its executor and reason. (Check: `tasks.md` section "Not for Kiro")
14. **Judges can open every cited path.** Every backticked repository path in `JUDGES.md`, the credits and the description draft shall exist and be export-visible. (Check: `python3 -B scripts/release/check_draft_paths.py <files>` prints `SUMMARY missing=0 excluded=0`)
