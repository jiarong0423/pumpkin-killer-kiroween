# Requirements: delivery plan (round 1)

Notation: EARS. "The plan" is `docs/plan_v1.md`. "A WP" is a work package, WP01 to WP62. "MVP" and "STRETCH" are the cutline values in the plan table. Dates are 2026 calendar dates in MM-DD form. Every item below can be checked by reading the plan or by running the command named in the item.

## Scope and deadline

1. The plan shall mark the contest deadline "to be confirmed by the owner" and shall cite the repo and web evidence for the date it found (`(not published)` line 3, the Devpost page).
2. The plan shall set day 0 = 11-09, working days 11-10 to 11-27, code freeze 11-27, and 11-28 and 11-29 for video and submission.
3. If the owner confirms a later deadline, then the plan shall treat the extra days as reserve and shall not move the 11-27 freeze without a new plan version.
4. The plan shall not edit `index.html`, `assets/`, `js/` or `scripts/`, shall not use git, and shall not create or download images.

## Work packages

5. The plan shall contain one table row per WP with: id, name, lane, owner, inputs, outputs, effort in hours, dependencies, start date, end date, verification, gate, risk (1 to 5) and cutline.
6. The plan shall group WPs into the eight lanes: Gameplay engine, Art (Colab), Audio, UI/HUD, Scenes, Balance and test, Kiro workflow evidence, Submission.
7. Each WP shall start on or after the end date of every WP it depends on (same day allowed).
8. Each MVP WP shall end by 11-27, except WP58, WP60, WP61 and WP62.
9. The verification cell of each WP shall name a command that exits 0 or a screenshot path, or shall state "could not verify here" with the reason.
10. Where a WP has an owner gate, the gate shall be one of the owner windows OW1 to OW8, and each window shall list one-word questions only.
11. The plan shall gate push and deploy (WP59) and the final submission (WP62) on the owner's explicit yes.
12. The Mermaid diagram shall contain every WP once, shall draw one edge per dependency in the table, and shall mark the critical path.

## Load and scope

13. The plan shall state main-line hours per WP and per day, and the daily sum shall equal the sum of the WP hours (147.7 h for MVP, 22 h for STRETCH).
14. The plan shall compare 1 v 1, 2 v 2 and 4 v 4 with hours and hours per day, and shall give a verdict for each.
15. If cumulative actual hours at a checkpoint exceed the plan's cumulative hours by 12 h or more, then the cut ladder shall be applied in the listed order.

## Art budget

16. The plan shall count the Colab images still needed by type, with the prompt file for each type, for MVP and STRETCH separately.
17. The plan shall compute Colab time as 2 minutes per image plus 5 minutes of model load per run, and shall report MVP minutes (242) and STRETCH minutes (131).
18. The plan shall order the Colab runs by the date a WP needs the image, so that no gameplay WP waits for an image it does not need yet.
19. The plan shall state a fallback for each late-art case and shall name the existing files in `assets/` that serve as fallback.
20. The plan shall not call for any image to be drawn, painted or generated by code.

## Checkpoints, risks, differences, questions

21. The plan shall contain 6 to 8 dated checkpoints; each shall give a 90-second demo description, a go rule and a no-go action.
22. The plan shall list 10 risks, each with evidence, mitigation and a trigger date.
23. The plan shall compare itself with `(not published)` and `docs/review_reconcile.md`, name each disagreement, and map every GAP row to a WP or to "not scheduled".
24. The plan shall list at most 8 questions, one line each, each with a stated default.
25. The plan shall list what could not be verified and what was assumed.

## Honesty

26. The plan shall not state that the browser game and the MCP server are connected.
27. The plan shall not state that Kiro generates images.
28. If a figure is a hand estimate, then the plan shall say so.
