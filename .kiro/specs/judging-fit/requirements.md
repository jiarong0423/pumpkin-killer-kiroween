# Requirements: judging fit

Notation: EARS. "The sheet" is `(not published)`. "The entry" is the Devpost submission plus the repo.

1. The sheet shall map each official criterion (Potential Value, Implementation, Quality and Design) to repo evidence, a gap and a fix.
2. The sheet shall cite a file path for every claim about what the repo shows.
3. If a Kiro feature has no file in the repo, then the sheet shall mark it "missing" and the entry shall not claim it.
4. The entry shall not state that Kiro generated images.
5. When the entry states a measured result, the entry shall use a figure from a run made within the same submission week, with the command that produced it.
6. If the README, steering or write-up disagree with each other about history or stack, then the disagreement shall be listed on the sheet and fixed before submission.
7. The demo video outline shall total under 3 minutes and each beat shall name the file or screen it shows.
8. While a feature is not built or not confirmed, the video and write-up shall not show it as working.
9. Before submission, the checklist shall be completed: public repo, OSI license, `.kiro/` committed and not ignored, video under 3 minutes on YouTube, authorised third-party assets, anonymous read-back, no `Co-Authored-By` trailer.
