## Resemblance note: the ghost nun (owner decision 2026-11-16)
An outside check (Gemini, per the owner) says the ghost nun resembles an existing film character. The owner judges it acceptable ("it is fine"). Recorded as a known, accepted risk. Features that drive the resemblance: black habit with a white wimple, a pale face with thin cracks, glowing yellow eyes. Cheap differentiators if the owner changes their mind (none applied): eye colour (cyan or amber instead of yellow), no face cracks, a Halloween element in the hands (candle or pumpkin lantern). Rules kept: the character is named "Silver Cross Sister" / "Ghost Nun" in English, the public text never names the film or its character, and the page shows no film material.
