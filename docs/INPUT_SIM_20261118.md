Reading (2026-11-18, after the first red-team review): the SHIPPED slingshot (row sling) takes its angle from the direction of the pull, so a 1 px tremor swings the angle by about 57.3 / pull-length degrees: 1.3 degrees at p95 in this model, above the 1 degree threshold, and worse for short pulls (2.5 degrees per px at a pull of 23 px). The owner reported in chat, after trying it, that the slingshot is fine (「剛剛測彈弓可以」, 2026-11-18; no test record or data exists, and the pull range of this row, 40 to 110 px, is narrower than the shipped firing range of about 25 to 120 px, where the p95 jitter is closer to 1.9 degrees); if it feels jumpy for short pulls the candidates are a smoothed angle that follows the pull length, or a longer minimum pull; nothing was changed because the owner accepts it as it is. The jitter column is a closed form (1 / radius, or 1 / px-per-degree), not a simulated value; the age column counts only lost events.

Not covered: real touch event rates and drops (measure on the phone), real learning, and the feel of a left thumb (the owner tries switchable versions).
