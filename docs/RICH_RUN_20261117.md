## Findings and suggestions (numbers above)
1. The page computer never fires the knight ultimate (0 of 10,008 matches; the placement is by hand, the computer has none). Knight 0.265 on the page against 0.401 on the engine. Implement a computer knight ultimate (place the three shield points on the remembered enemy paths) or lift the knight by about 5 percent hp; do it before judging the knight.
2. Reaper is the strongest on both ends (0.617 / 0.617), bride (0.42 / 0.41) and bat (0.44 / 0.44) the weakest apart from the page knight: trim the reaper about 5 percent damage, add about 5 percent to bride and bat damage.
3. Duels are long: mean 18 to 21 rounds, 3.6 to 7.8 percent end at the cap 40; easy against easy (target 0.15 hits per throw) runs about 32 rounds. Raise the easy target to about 0.20 or lower the duel cap to 30.
4. A left-side edge remains on the page at equal tiers: 54.2 / 57.7 / 57.6 percent (engine 50.5 / 54.8 / 51.9, where red always opens). Find the cause (opener coin, start slots, left knobs) before more balance changes.
5. Teams of 2 or 3 end by the cap in 2.5 to 5.5 percent of matches and the ramp starts at round 13: the late-game ramp could start at round 10 so fewer matches reach the cap.
6. Measure the real seconds per turn by playing (the owner) and add crate pick-up counts before the next full run.
