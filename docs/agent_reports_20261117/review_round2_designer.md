## 3. Ratings (now; projected after the pack is [P], not human-tested) and ranked weaknesses
Depth 5 (6): one real decision per turn (power against wind; angle 50-75 interchangeable in the sims), 3 once-per-match skills, 2 ults, forgiving 140 x 190 box. Fairness 6 (7): hero spread 44-58, tempo 59.6/40.4, aim 93 percent. Pacing 7 for duels (8.5 rounds), 3 for teams (7). Team play 3 (5): units are HP bars, one thrower per round, 71-75 percent of winners end with 1 unit. Replayability (耐玩度) 5 (6.5): the skill ladder is real, but hero pool and one-shot-per-turn loop repeat.
Weaknesses: 1 team length 20-29 rounds; 2 aim gap 90-94 percent; 3 shallow decision space; 4 early lead holds 68-70; 5 ultimates sparse (0.6-1.3 per short match) and unequal 2.8x; 6 hero imbalance (skeleton duel, bride team); 7 tempo 59.6; 8 fence irrelevant; 9 page and Python engine disagree (round 1 item 5).
3-minute video will show: arc, wind, hit and HP bars, art, one or two ultimates, a 1v1 cut to 6-8 rounds. It will not show: balance, comebacks, how long teams take, the skill curve, match 10, relay/MCP. A 5-minute judge session fits one duel (8.5 rounds, about 3.5-4 min [P, 25 s per round]) or a third of a baseline 3v3 (20-24 rounds, 9-10 min [P]); with A1+A2 a 3v3 is 9-11 rounds, about 4 min [P]. They will see 0-1 ultimates, no fence break, at most 1-2 crates.

## 4. Replayability suggestions
Before 2026-11-25: (1) A1+A2 and D (a few constants; makes 3v3 playable in a session). (2) B1 blast and B2 underdog bonus first (visible, cheap), B3 after a playtest. (3) C pip cost 2 and ult normalisation. (4) HUD: round counter, HP-lead line, "underdog +x percent" badge. (5) Daily seed match (seed already fixes wind and start x): same seed for everyone, score = HP left, share a code. (6) Skeleton/wizard hp tweak.
Later: squad action (every living unit throws at about 0.55 damage, or a swap/support action) so 3 units are a squad; hero roles (healer, tank, area); per-match modifiers (weather exists; fence height or gap as a variable); hero ban/pick for ranked; replay by seed; AI levels as a campaign; the pity crate for team matches.
