# Dock asset requests for Colab

Images that would make the bottom dock look premium. They must be generated on Colab (Qwen-Image-Edit) and never faked with code.
`css/dock.css` already draws a complete dock without them; each image replaces one CSS-drawn surface through `background-image` or `--portrait-img`.

Conventions (same as `colab/jobs.json`): flat solid magenta background `#FF00FF` for anything that needs transparency (chroma-keyed afterwards), no text, no watermark, no cast shadow outside the object,
dark-fantasy 2D cartoon art with thick clean outlines, matching the four unit images. Palette: aged stone `#2a2337`, wood `#4a2e1a`, candle gold `#ffcf5a`, pumpkin orange `#ff8a1f`, spectral cyan `#5df2ee`, violet `#a98bff`.
Sizes are the generated size; the dock shows them at about one third to one half, so they stay sharp on a 2x screen.

Ordered by visual impact (1 = biggest change).
