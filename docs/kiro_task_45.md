## What the arena must implement (phase A first, then phase B; say clearly in the report which phases are finished)
PHASE A (must work and be tested):
- Field 1920 wide, GROUND_Y 860, fence at x 960 (width 130, height from index.html), physics identical to index.html and scripts/sim_balance.js (fixed step 1/60, gravity 980, wind acceleration 72 px/s^2 per wind unit, wind in [-4, 4] changing each round by at most 2 units, launch speed power*13.8, hit box |dx| <= 70 and a 190 px body box, head zone = the upper 25 percent).
- 4 v 4: each side picks 4 of the 7 classes (no duplicates); start positions on the allowed zones (left 160 to 760, right 1160 to 1760) at least 160 px apart; HP and damage from colab/class_stats_v2.json; turn structure from docs/roster_design.md as corrected by docs/review_reconcile.md (state your choice in the report: e.g. side phases, one unit acts per turn); 12 s turn timer is NOT simulated, instead a stunned unit loses part of its action budget (state how); the Move action (dice 1 to 6 steps of 36 px, direction chosen, once per turn, can still attack after).
- Normal attacks: skeleton javelin (frost curse, bleed), wizard cannon (poison, chance fire patch from a public seed), cat-bat ONE shuriken (slow = cannot move next turn), knight bouncing shield (die 1 to 6, split damage, knockback), bride sound wave (hits the nearest unit to the wave centre, poison), hunchback axe (bleed, knockback 50 px), reaper skull (bleed, execute bonus).
- Statuses with a cap of 2 statuses per unit, head crit x1.25 with stun, knockback rule (pushed along x away from the thrower, stopped by the fence, clamped to the zones, never onto another unit), energy of 3 pips through js/ultimate.js (use the real module).
- PUBLIC STATE per unit: positions, HP, statuses, pips, ultimates left, wind, round, the last 4 recorded trajectories (history), the opponent's visible effects. HIDDEN: the knight's floating shield and its A-B-C points (revealed 1.4 s worth of events only when it blocks); not in the public state of the opponent.
- COMMAND-LINE INTERFACE so a sub-agent can play with plain Bash: `node (not published) new --seed 7 --a skeleton,wizard,bat,knight --b bride,hunchback,reaper,skeleton --out $F` creates a match file; `node (not published) view --file $F --unit A1` prints the PUBLIC JSON for that unit's controller (whose turn it is, legal actions, its hidden own info); `node (not published) act --file $F --unit A1 --json '{"move":{"dir":1},"angle":54,"power":70}'` applies the action, advances the match (other units that are not controlled by an agent are driven by a built-in bounded-error bot) and prints the result events; `node (not published) status --file $F` prints the winner or "ongoing". Everything deterministic from the seed, one JSON file as the whole state, illegal actions return a clear error and change nothing.
PHASE B (after A is green; mark unfinished parts as TODO in the report, do not fake them): the seven ultimates as in docs/ultimate_mapping.md and the owner log (cat volley 1.5 x / 5, skeleton triple shot +5 percent each with an all-three crit, wizard explosion + 13-step molotov fire, bride precise-hit bat swarm x1.3 indiscriminate, hunchback boomerang that returns only if it hits the LAST unit, reaper skull then three slashes), the A-B-C hidden shield, burning ground.
