What the mockup shows (written by the main line, who looked at it): rich stylised game art with strong depth in layers (near dark frame, mid props, far skyline), soft volumetric lighting and mist, glowing lights (moon, candle flames, lanterns, ghost fire) that spill coloured light on nearby surfaces, warm orange against cold shadows, fine surface texture on stone, wood and cloth, characters with dark but soft outlines, detailed faces and costumes, small sparkles and particles, polished mobile-game concept-painting finish.
Use in every prompt: "rich stylised game art in the look of a polished Halloween mobile-game concept painting: layered depth, soft volumetric lighting and mist, glowing light sources that tint nearby surfaces, fine texture, soft dark outlines, no flat cel shading". Never say "flat three-tone cel shading" any more. Sprites still sit on a flat magenta background so they can be keyed.
