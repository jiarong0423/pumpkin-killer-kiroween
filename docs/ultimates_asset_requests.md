# Ultimate and effect asset requests for Colab (Kiro task 35)

Images needed for the ultimates, area attacks and showpiece effects in `docs/ultimates_design.md`. They must be made on Colab (Qwen-Image-Edit) and never faked with code. Nothing here has been generated or checked; I cannot see images. At most 16, highest impact first.

Conventions (same as `docs/dock_asset_requests.md` and `docs/airdrop_asset_requests.md`): flat solid magenta background `#FF00FF` (chroma-keyed afterwards), no text, no watermark, no cast shadow outside the object, dark-fantasy 2D cartoon art with thick clean outlines. Palette: stone `#2a2337`, wood `#4a2e1a`, candle gold `#ffcf5a`, pumpkin orange `#ff8a1f`, spectral cyan `#5df2ee`, violet `#a98bff`, poison green `#5dff4a`. Anything that has a direction (spear, shield, shuriken) is drawn once facing LEFT and mirrored by the game for the other team.

**Not requested because they exist or are already requested:** ghost-fire, rune circle, stardust, spirit mist, impact flash, poison bubbles (existing effect sprites); `proj_spear`, `proj_pumpkin`, `shuriken`, `small_shield` (existing weapons); the six airdrop icons; `portrait_cutin_<class>_{charge,shout,hit}` (item 5 of `docs/dock_asset_requests.md`, used for the ultimate cut-in). The programmatic effects (rings, sparks, dust, wisps, the vignette, glows) are code, not images.

Where a prompt names a reference, feed that existing file as the reference image so the style matches.
